Two kinds of fixes

Some style fixes are always right. Others depend on what you meant. Buzzword handles them differently.

Safe fixes become suggestions

Dates, times, numbers and state names that are always written one way get added as regular Google Docs suggestions. Accept or reject them like any other edit.

  • 6pm 6 p.m. Times
  • November 24 Nov. 24 Dates

Judgment calls become cards

If a fix depends on context, like whether 8:30 is morning or evening, Buzzword leaves your text alone. It adds a card to its panel with a short Why? note, a button to find the spot in your doc, and an option to ignore it.

  • Times with no a.m. or p.m. AP
  • "Burlingame High School" where just "Burlingame" will do The B

Rules, not AI

Every check is a written rule. Nothing is sent to an AI model, and the same draft gets the same results every time.

Only the story body

It checks the text between the "Article Draft" heading and the "Otter link + Notes" section of The B's template. The header table and your source notes are never read or changed.

No means no

Reject a suggestion or ignore a card and Buzzword won't bring it up again on that document.

How to use it

Install Buzzword once, then run it whenever a draft is ready for a pass.

  1. Open your draft

    Open a story that uses The B's Article Draft template in Google Docs.

  2. Click the bee

    Buzz sits in the bottom-right corner of the doc. Click it to open the panel.

  3. Pick what to check

    Choose Check whole story, or select a paragraph and choose Check selection.

  4. Review the fixes

    Accept or reject the suggestions in the doc, and work through the cards in the panel.

  5. Check again

    Run it once more to confirm everything's clean. Rejected fixes stay gone.

The first time, Google will ask you to let Buzzword read and suggest edits to your Docs. That's how it adds suggestions.

Your drafts stay yours

  • Your text is checked inside your browser.
  • It's sent to only one place: Google's Docs API, to add suggestions to the same doc.
  • There's no Buzzword server, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • Buzzword saves settings, document IDs, suggestion IDs, hashes of flags you rejected or ignored, and accept/reject counts per rule. It never saves your document text.
  • This website has no analytics or tracking either.
